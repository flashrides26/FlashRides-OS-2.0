# 9. Architectural Lessons Learned

The review identified the following validated architectural practices:

- The Governance Artifact Template successfully transferred from the Constitution to the Policy Framework.
- Cross-referencing authoritative governance artifacts prevents duplication while preserving artifact focus.
- Separating **Relationships** from **Navigation** improves governance clarity and repository usability.
- Framework artifacts function as governance architecture rather than comprehensive governance documentation.
- Single-responsibility governance artifacts reduce maintenance complexity and minimize the risk of conflicting interpretations.
- Maintaining a single authoritative artifact for each governance concept supports long-term consistency and repository evolution.

These lessons should be retained as architectural evidence for future governance domains and considered during development of the Governance Standards.
