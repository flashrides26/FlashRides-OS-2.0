# 2. Purpose

The Policy Classification Framework establishes the organizational methodology for classifying policies within FlashRides-OS.

Its purpose is to ensure that every organizational policy is classified consistently according to the governance subject it regulates rather than the organizational function responsible for implementing, administering, or enforcing the policy.

Consistent policy classification promotes governance clarity, preserves single-source authority, simplifies navigation, and supports long-term scalability of the governance repository.

---

# 3. Governance Responsibility

This framework governs:

- Policy classification methodology
- Policy domains
- Policy classification principles
- Policy numbering by domain
- Repository organization for policy artifacts

This framework does not govern:

- Policy approval
- Policy lifecycle
- Policy authority
- Policy templates
- Policy review
- Policy exceptions
- Policy compliance
- Policy retirement

Those subjects are governed by their respective Policy Framework artifacts.

---

# 4. Classification Framework

Policies shall be classified according to the governance subject they regulate.

Classification shall not be determined by:

- Organizational ownership
- Departmental responsibility
- Operational implementation
- Administrative oversight
- Training responsibility

## Policy Domain

A **Policy Domain** is a governance classification that groups policies regulating the same organizational subject.

Policy Domains exist to:

- Preserve governance clarity
- Establish a single authoritative location for related policies
- Support consistent repository organization
- Eliminate ambiguity regarding policy classification

Each policy shall exist within one authoritative Policy Domain.

Organizational documents that require a policy shall reference the authoritative policy rather than reproducing its governance content.

Policy Domains currently include:

- Customer Policies
- Financial Policies
- Governance Policies
- Operational Policies
- Safety Policies
- Technology Policies

Additional Policy Domains may be established through the governance approval process when organizational growth requires new governance classifications.

---

# 5. Organizational Impact

Consistent policy classification provides:

- Clear governance responsibility
- Consistent repository organization
- Simplified policy discovery
- Reduced duplication
- Improved maintainability
- Preservation of single-source governance authority

The framework supports sustainable organizational growth by ensuring policies remain organized according to governance purpose rather than organizational structure.

# 7. Maintenance

The Organization Steward shall periodically review this framework to ensure policy classifications remain consistent with organizational governance.

Amendments shall be approved through the established governance approval process.

New Policy Domains shall only be established when existing classifications no longer adequately represent organizational governance responsibilities.
