# Purpose

The Constitutional Index serves as the authoritative navigation document for the FlashRides Governance Constitution.

It identifies each constitutional artifact, its publication order, constitutional responsibility, and relationship to the overall constitutional architecture.

The Index supports constitutional navigation without replacing or interpreting the constitutional artifacts themselves.

# Repository Structure

The Governance Constitution forms the constitutional layer of the FlashRides governance system.

Derivative governance artifacts—including policies, standards, procedures, operational guidance, templates, and other supporting governance documentation—derive their authority from one or more constitutional artifacts.

Derivative artifacts shall remain subordinate to the Constitution and shall not establish independent constitutional authority.

---

# Maintenance

The Constitutional Index should be updated whenever constitutional artifacts are added, removed, renamed, renumbered, or assigned revised constitutional responsibilities.

The Index serves as the authoritative navigation document for the constitutional layer and should remain synchronized with the Governance Constitution at all times.

---

# Navigation

Readers beginning their study of the Governance Constitution should first read the README before continuing to the Organization Constitution.

The constitutional artifacts should thereafter be read in publication order unless a specific constitutional artifact is being consulted.

Together, the README, Constitutional Index, and constitutional artifacts provide a complete framework for understanding, navigating, and applying the FlashRides Governance Constitution.
