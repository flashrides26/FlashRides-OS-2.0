# 1. Purpose

The Constitutional Glossary establishes the authoritative definitions of constitutional terminology used throughout the FlashRides Constitution.

It promotes consistent understanding by ensuring that constitutional language is interpreted uniformly across all constitutional and derivative artifacts.

The Constitutional Glossary preserves the shared constitutional vocabulary upon which governance, interpretation, judgment, and stewardship depend.

---

# 2. Constitutional Responsibility

The Constitutional Glossary is the authoritative constitutional artifact defining the meaning of constitutional terminology used throughout the FlashRides Constitution.

It derives its authority from the Organization Constitution and shall remain consistent with the constitutional identity, purpose, philosophy, interpretation, methodology, and governance established therein.

The Constitutional Glossary defines constitutional language without creating constitutional authority, modifying constitutional meaning, or prescribing operational implementation.
