# 1. Purpose

The Constitutional Decision Framework establishes the constitutional methodology by which significant organizational judgment shall be exercised throughout FlashRides.

While the Guiding Principles establish the enduring philosophy of leadership, the Constitutional Decision Framework provides the required sequence for faithfully applying that philosophy whenever constitutional judgment is required.

The framework promotes consistency, transparency, stewardship, and organizational trust by ensuring that constitutional decisions are evaluated through a common methodology.

---

# 2. Constitutional Responsibility

The Constitutional Decision Framework is the authoritative constitutional artifact defining the required methodology for exercising constitutional judgment.

It derives its authority from the Organization Constitution and shall remain consistent with the constitutional identity, purpose, philosophy, interpretation, governance, and constitutional language established therein.

The Constitutional Decision Framework governs the order in which constitutional considerations are evaluated but does not replace constitutional interpretation, leadership responsibility, discernment, or accountability.

---

# 3. Constitutional Decision Framework

Constitutional judgment shall be exercised through the following sequence.

Each stage builds upon the previous stage.

No stage may invalidate or bypass a preceding stage.

---

## Stage 1 — Constitutional Alignment

### Constitutional Question

> **May this decision be made?**

### Purpose

Determine whether the proposed decision is consistent with the Constitution, as faithfully understood through the Constitutional Interpretation Framework.

Options that conflict with the Constitution shall not proceed to subsequent stages.

---

## Stage 2 — Mission-Centered Judgment

### Constitutional Question

> **Should this decision be made?**

### Purpose

Among constitutionally permissible options, determine which most faithfully advances the Mission while remaining consistent with the Vision, Core Values, Organizational Axioms, and Guiding Principles.

---

## Stage 3 — Faithful Exercise

### Constitutional Question

> **How should this decision be exercised?**

### Purpose

Exercise the selected decision with integrity, respect, humility, and faithful stewardship toward all people affected by the decision.

The manner in which a decision is exercised is itself a constitutional responsibility.

---

## Stage 4 — Stewardship Through Learning

### Constitutional Question

> **What should be learned?**

### Purpose

Evaluate the outcome of the decision to strengthen future judgment, improve stewardship, and cultivate organizational wisdom.

Faithful organizations continually learn without compromising constitutional truth.

---

# 4. Organizational Impact

The Constitutional Decision Framework promotes consistency in constitutional judgment throughout FlashRides.

It encourages leaders to evaluate decisions through the same constitutional methodology while preserving appropriate discretion for individual circumstances.

By establishing a shared decision methodology, the framework strengthens organizational trust, improves leadership consistency, and supports faithful stewardship across all levels of the organization.

The framework is intended to guide judgment rather than replace thoughtful leadership.

# 6. Maintenance

## Purpose

The Constitutional Decision Framework shall be maintained as the authoritative constitutional artifact defining the methodology for constitutional judgment.

---

## Stewardship

The Constitutional Decision Framework shall be maintained under the authority of the Organization Constitution.

The Organization Owner retains constitutional authority over the Constitutional Decision Framework, while the Organization Steward is responsible for preserving its clarity, integrity, and constitutional consistency.

---

## Review

The Constitutional Decision Framework should be reviewed periodically to confirm it continues to faithfully express the constitutional methodology for organizational judgment.

Periodic review does not imply revision.

Changes should occur only when necessary to preserve constitutional consistency or more faithfully express the enduring methodology of constitutional judgment.

---

## Constitutional Evolution

Any modification to the Constitutional Decision Framework shall occur exclusively through the Constitutional Amendment Framework.

Constitutional evolution should be deliberate, infrequent, and consistent with the identity, purpose, philosophy, interpretation, governance, and constitutional language established by the Organization Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Integrity

The Constitutional Decision Framework shall remain timeless, methodological, and focused exclusively on defining the sequence for constitutional judgment.

Responsibilities assigned to other constitutional artifacts shall not be duplicated within the Constitutional Decision Framework.
