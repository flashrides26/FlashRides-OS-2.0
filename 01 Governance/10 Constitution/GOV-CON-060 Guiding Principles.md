# GOV-CON-060 — Constitutional Decision Framework

**Status:** Draft 1.0 (Provisionally Complete)  
**Classification:** Governance Constitution  
**Authority:** FlashRides Constitution

---

# Purpose

The Constitutional Decision Framework establishes the required sequence for exercising constitutional judgment. It provides a repeatable process that ensures every decision is evaluated consistently, faithfully, and in alignment with the FlashRides Constitution.

---

# Discovery

The Mission establishes *why* FlashRides exists.

The Axioms establish *what must always be true.*

The Core Values establish *who we must be.*

The Guiding Principles establish *how constitutional judgment is exercised.*

A constitutional framework is therefore required to establish the order in which those principles are applied whenever judgment is required.

---

# Constitutional Question

> **How shall constitutional judgment be exercised from beginning to end?**

---

# Constitutional Definition

The Constitutional Decision Framework is the required sequence through which all constitutional judgment shall be exercised. Each step builds upon the previous one, ensuring that decisions remain constitutionally aligned, mission-centered, respectful, and teachable.

---

# Constitutional Framework

## Step 1 — Constitutional Alignment

**Question**

> Is this aligned with the Constitution?

> Does this conflict with any constitutional principle or obligation?

**Purpose**

Eliminate all constitutionally impermissible options before judgment continues.

---

## Step 2 — Mission-Centered Judgment

**Question**

> Which constitutionally aligned option most faithfully advances the Mission?

**Purpose**

Select the constitutionally permissible option that most faithfully fulfills the FlashRides Mission.

---

## Step 3 — Respectful Judgment

**Question**

> How should this decision be exercised toward those affected?

**Purpose**

Ensure constitutional decisions preserve dignity, trust, and relationships while remaining faithful to the Mission and Core Values.

---

## Step 4 — Teachable Judgment

**Question**

> What does this decision teach us, and how should our judgment improve?

**Purpose**

Promote continual learning, humility, and faithful improvement through constitutional stewardship.

---

# Constitutional Sequence

Every constitutional decision shall proceed in the following order:

1. Constitutional Alignment
2. Mission-Centered Judgment
3. Respectful Judgment
4. Teachable Judgment

No step may bypass or invalidate a previous step.

---

# Architectural Boundaries

The Constitutional Decision Framework does not:

- define the Mission;
- establish Axioms;
- define Core Values;
- replace operational procedures;
- replace leadership authority.

Its sole purpose is to govern the constitutional exercise of judgment.

---

# Constitutional Failure Mode

Without the Constitutional Decision Framework:

- constitutional principles may be applied inconsistently;
- judgment may become subjective;
- leaders may reach different conclusions from the same constitutional foundation;
- organizational trust may erode through inconsistent reasoning.

---

# Constitutional Evidence

The Constitutional Decision Framework emerged through the constitutional discovery of the Guiding Principles.

Each Guiding Principle answered a unique constitutional question:

1. May this decision be made?
2. Which permissible decision best advances the Mission?
3. How should the decision be exercised?
4. What should be learned from the outcome?

Together these discoveries formed a complete constitutional model for decision-making.

# Constitutional Summary

The Constitutional Decision Framework transforms the Guiding Principles from individual standards into a unified model for constitutional judgment.

It ensures that every significant decision is evaluated through the same constitutional sequence:

- Align with the Constitution.
- Advance the Mission.
- Exercise judgment respectfully.
- Learn and improve continuously.

This framework provides FlashRides with a consistent, repeatable, and constitutionally faithful method for exercising judgment at every level of the organization.
